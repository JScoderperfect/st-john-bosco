Who Was St John Bosco?

St John Bosco was an Italian Catholic priest who dedicated his life to educating and supporting young people, especially those who were poor or disadvantaged.

He believed that young people needed kindness, guidance, education and faith to help them succeed in life.

His Life Timeline

1815 — His Birth

John Bosco was born in Becchi, Italy, on 16 August.

1841 — Became a Priest

He was ordained as a Catholic priest.

1846 — Helping Young People

He established his work with young people in Valdocco, Turin.

1859 — The Salesians

He founded the Salesian Society to continue his mission.

1934 — Became a Saint

Pope Pius XI declared him a saint.
